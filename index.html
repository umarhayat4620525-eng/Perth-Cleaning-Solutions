Our Story

Why We Started

Perth Eco Clean Co. was founded on a straightforward idea: that a cleaning service should do three things well — show up on time, use products that are safe for your family and the environment, and charge a price that is fair and transparent.

We are a locally owned Perth business. We live here, we work here, and we care about the community we operate in. When you book with us, you are not dealing with a call centre or a national franchise — you are dealing with people who take genuine pride in the work.

Why Eco-Friendly?

We made the decision from day one to use only biodegradable, environmentally safe cleaning products across all our services. Not because it is a marketing point — but because we believe it is the right way to operate in a city as naturally beautiful as Perth.

Where We Started

We launched with two services — wheelie bin cleaning and end-of-lease cleaning — and we chose to focus on those rather than spread ourselves thin. We would rather be excellent at two things than average at ten. As we grow our team, we will expand into window cleaning, pressure washing, and more — but only when we can deliver them to the same standard.

Our Team

Perth Eco Clean Co. is run by a small, dedicated local team. Every job is carried out by trained professionals who follow a consistent checklist and take accountability for the result. We do not cut corners, and we do not disappear when something needs following up.
