❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know. Can't find an answer? Give us a call or send us a message.

❓
How does the bin cleaning service work?
+
We schedule your bin clean on the same day as your council rubbish collection. Once your bins have been emptied, our team arrives, gives them a thorough high-pressure hot water clean, applies an eco-friendly deodoriser, and returns them to your property — all within the same day.
How often should I get my bins cleaned?
+
For most households, a monthly clean is sufficient. If you have a large family or generate a lot of food waste, a fortnightly clean is recommended. We offer both options at affordable rates.
Will the end of lease clean guarantee my bond back?
+
We follow REIWA standards and our clean is designed to meet property manager expectations. If anything is flagged by your agent, we'll return and re-clean those areas at no extra charge.
Do I need to be home during the clean?
+
For bin cleaning, you don't need to be home at all. For end of lease cleans, we recommend you or your representative be available, though it's not always required.
What areas in Perth do you service?
+
We currently service Perth and surrounding suburbs. If you're unsure whether we cover your area, please contact us or fill out the quote form.
Do you use eco-friendly products?
+
Yes! All our cleaning products are environmentally safe and biodegradable. We care about the Perth environment and always choose products that are effective without harming your family or the earth.
How do I pay?
+
We accept bank transfer, cash, and major credit/debit cards. For regular service plans, we can arrange a simple monthly payment.
Can I bundle bin cleaning with end of lease?
+
Absolutely! We offer bundle deals for customers who need both services. Select "Bundle" in the quote form and we'll put together a combined price.
